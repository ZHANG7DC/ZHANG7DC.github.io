Cross-survey generation, modality imputation, and galaxy morphology.

Different telescopes offer complementary views of the same sky. Our research develops machine-learning methods to connect these views, infer missing observations, and learn useful galaxy representations with limited human supervision.

01 / GENERATIVE MODELINGKDD 2026

AS-Bridge: A Bidirectional Generative Framework Bridging Next-Generation Astronomical Surveys

Dichang Zhang, Yixuan Shao, Simon Birrer, Dimitris Samaras

A bidirectional generative framework for translating between ground- and space-based survey observations. A stochastic Brownian bridge models conditional image distributions, supporting probabilistic prediction of missing observations and inter-survey rare-event detection.

02 / MODALITY IMPUTATIONNeurIPS 2026 · Accepted

COSMIO: A Benchmark for Cross-Survey Modality Imputation

Dichang Zhang, Yixuan Shao, Jiali Cui, Haotian Yin, Yuanpeng Liu, Jiaqi Deng, Xiang Gao, Zhiqiang Lao, Heather Yu, Simon Birrer, Dimitris Samaras

A benchmark for cross-survey modality imputation: studying how observations from available surveys can help infer missing views of the same astronomical objects.

Accepted for publication at NeurIPS 2026.

03 / GALAXY MORPHOLOGYarXiv 2026

A General-Purpose VLM Can Teach an Astronomy Foundation Model to Better Recognize Galaxy Morphology

Dichang Zhang, Jiaqi Deng, Yixuan Shao, Yuanpeng Liu, Jiali Cui, Zhiqiang Lao, Heather Yu, Liang Peng, Simon Birrer, Dimitris Samaras

A general-purpose vision-language model provides weak supervision for an astronomy foundation model. The approach improves galaxy morphology recognition with limited human labels, while retaining a role for human annotation and validation.
