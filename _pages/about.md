          I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Stony Brook University, advised by
          <a href="https://www3.cs.stonybrook.edu/~samaras/">Prof. Dimitris Samaras</a>. I also collaborate with
          <a href="https://www.stonybrook.edu/commcms/physics/people/_profiles/birrers">Prof. Simon Birrer</a> in the Department of Physics and Astronomy. I have also been a visiting doctoral student at EPFL with
          <a href="https://people.epfl.ch/pascal.fua">Prof. Pascal Fua</a>. Before that, I completed my B.S. in Data Science and Honors Computer Science at the University of Michigan, where I worked with
          <a href="https://cs.nyu.edu/~fouhey/">Prof. David Fouhey</a>. I was a research intern at Futurewei Technologies from February to August 2026.
        

**Research interests:** computer vision, machine learning, and astronomy image analysis.
